About / How to use

Choose a hold cap and press Start. The charts share a clock. Sloped lines show travelling and flat lines show waiting or serving passengers. The two School gaps are measured when buses start boarding.

Each stop serves one bus at a time. Passengers who are waiting can board at the start of service. New passengers arrive until Bus 3 dispatches at 13 minutes. All admitted passengers finish their journeys.

Only Bus 2 can be deliberately held. Riders already aboard spend that time waiting. The table reports Hold Bus 2 minus No holding for the same riders. The notebook estimates the average effects across repeated matched runs.
