About these runs

All panels share the same bus schedule, passengers and road travel times. At 1×, one real second advances the clock by one simulated second.

The preview uses a departure gap of 1 min 40 s by default. The notebook experiment and both replays use 1 min 20 s. Holding always aims for a 1 min 20 s release gap before Market.

Published link times come from Herath et al. The original road disruption is a separate replay. Counts update when passengers board or alight at the start of service.
