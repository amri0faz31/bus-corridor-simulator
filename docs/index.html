About these runs

All panels share the same bus schedule, passengers and road travel times. At 1×, one real second advances the clock by one simulated second.

Rising lines show buses travelling. Flat lines show time at a stop or its approach. Yellow marks road delay, light green marks deliberate holding and blue marks queueing.

Holding aims to keep release gaps before Market close to the scheduled departure gap. Random passengers arrive until Bus 3 dispatches. The buses then finish serving those passengers.

Counts update at passenger arrivals and at the start of boarding or alighting. Platform waits average boarded passengers, and journey times average completed trips.
